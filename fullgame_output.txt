Before all of this, the hero was only human.
He lived in Karakura Town, a quiet place that never truly felt ordinary.
One day, after crossing the border between life and death, he became a Shinigami.
No ancient blessing was given to him. No legendary power awakened inside his soul.
He had only his will, his blade, and the duty placed before him.

Karakura Town is now drowning in restless spirits.
Hollows have begun to appear across the streets, blocking pure souls from reaching the afterlife.
These Hollows were not always monsters. Many were once gentle spirits, twisted by a darker force.
To save them, the hero must defeat them. Not out of hatred, but to end their suffering.

But Karakura Town is only the beginning...
